# Result Dictionary & Metric Definitions

**Canonical Repository:** [https://github.com/AkashKundu114/clustering-stability-discordance](https://github.com/AkashKundu114/clustering-stability-discordance)
**Submitted Manuscript Reference:** *Stability, Accuracy, and Feature-Gated Bipartite Consensus Under Noise*
**Authors:** Kalyan Kumar Das, Akash Kundu, Anirban Mukhopadhyay

This dictionary provides standard definitions, mathematical formulas, inferential units, and exact numerical values for all primary, secondary, and diagnostic metrics reported in the submitted manuscript.

---

## 1. Primary Empirical Metrics

### Subsample Stability ($\bar{S}$)
- **Mathematical Definition:** Mean pairwise Adjusted Rand Index (ARI) between cluster assignments evaluated across overlapping sample subsets:
  $$S_{ij} = \text{ARI}\left(P_i|_{I_i \cap I_j}, P_j|_{I_i \cap I_j}\right), \quad \bar{S} = \frac{1}{\binom{B}{2}} \sum_{1 \le i < j \le B} S_{ij}$$
  where $B = 30$ stratified $80\%$ subsamples per cell ($\binom{30}{2} = 435$ pairs).
- **Reported Manuscript Value:** `0.510` (Fresh grand mean across 72 cells)
- **Independently Recalculated Fresh Value:** `0.509811523966`
- **Historical Value:** `0.516586062082`
- **Inferential Note:** Pairwise ARIs sharing subsample partitions are statistically dependent ($\text{Cov}(S_{1,2}, S_{1,3}) \neq 0$). The primary inferential unit is the cell-level mean ($N=72$) or cohort block ($N=6$), not $N=435$ or $N=31{,}320$.

### External Reference-Label Accuracy ($\bar{A}$)
- **Mathematical Definition:** Mean Adjusted Rand Index between predicted subsample cluster assignments and external diagnostic reference labels:
  $$\bar{A} = \frac{1}{B} \sum_{i=1}^B \text{ARI}\left(P_i, Y|_{I_i}\right)$$
  where $Y$ denotes the operational reference categories (clinical cancer subtypes).
- **Reported Manuscript Value:** `0.053` (Fresh grand mean across 72 cells)
- **Independently Recalculated Fresh Value:** `0.052522289401`
- **Historical Value:** `0.052311684347`
- **Interpretation:** External reference labels provide an operational diagnostic benchmark, not an infallible biological ground truth.

### Stability–Accuracy Discordance Gap ($\bar{\Delta}$)
- **Mathematical Definition:** Arithmetic difference between subsample stability and external reference accuracy:
  $$\bar{\Delta} = \bar{S} - \bar{A}$$
- **Reported Manuscript Values:**
  - Fresh Unconditional Mean Gap: `+0.457` (Recalculated: `+0.457289234565`)
  - Fresh Median Gap: `+0.442` (Recalculated: `+0.442304117680`)
  - Historical Mean Gap: `+0.464` (Recalculated: `+0.464274377735`)
  - Historical Median Gap: `+0.448` (Recalculated: `+0.448406816942`)
  - Historical 60 Non-Degenerate Cells Gap: `+0.485` (Recalculated: `+0.485376175653`)

### Column-Permuted Null Stability ($\bar{S}_{\text{null}}$)
- **Mathematical Definition:** Subsample stability evaluated on synthetic null controls generated by permuting each feature column independently (breaking multivariate coordinate covariance while preserving univariate marginal distributions).
- **Reported Manuscript Value:** `0.007` (Grand mean across 72 cells)
- **Independently Recalculated Fresh Value:** `0.006608106823`
- **Historical Value:** `0.006620228373`
- **Interpretation:** Proves that high empirical stability in clinical cohorts reflects genuine geometric concentration along data-driven coordinates, rather than mathematical artifacts of subsampling.

## 2. Discordance Filtering Rules & Subsets

### Rule (i) - Pre-Specified Numeric Discordance Filter
- **Criteria:** $\bar{S} \ge 0.50$ AND $\bar{A} \le 0.05$.
- **Cell Count:** Exactly **20 cells** pass out of 72.
- **Recalculated Metrics:** Mean $\bar{S} = 0.723201$, Mean $\bar{A} = 0.004052$, Mean $\bar{\Delta} = +0.719149$.

### Rule (i') - Post-Hoc Non-Degeneracy Filter
- **Criteria:** Passes Rule (i) AND smallest cluster contains $\ge 5\%$ of subsample points in $\ge 27$ of 30 subsample repeats ($\min N_c / N \ge 0.05$).
- **Cell Count:** Exactly **18 cells** pass (filters out `UngatedAC` on Colon [25/30 passed] and `K-Means` on CNS [24/30 passed]).
- **Timing Status:** Introduced post-hoc after observing trivial single-point cluster collapse in CoreHalo on Prostate.
- **Fresh Descriptive Means:**
  - Mean Stability: `0.718875` (Manuscript: `0.719`)
  - Mean External ARI: `0.004231` (Manuscript: `0.004`)
  - Mean Discordance Gap: `+0.714644` (Manuscript: `+0.715`)
- **Historical Means:** Mean $\bar{S} = 0.720011$, Mean $\bar{A} = 0.003873$.

### Rule (ii) - Pre-Specified Excess Stability Criterion
- **Criteria:** $\bar{S} - \bar{S}_{\text{null}} \ge 0.10$.
- **Result:** Satisfied by all 20 Rule (i) cells.

### Rule (iii) - Multi-Cohort Persistence Core
- **Criteria:** Algorithms satisfying Rule (i') across $\ge 3$ distinct cohorts.
- **Qualifying Algorithms:** `FCAG` (CNS, Colon, Prostate), `FCM` (CNS, Colon, Ovarian), `FastMVC` (CNS, Colon, Prostate) - totaling **9 cells**.
- **9-Cell Core Descriptive Means:** Mean $\bar{S} = \mathbf{0.683764}$ (`0.684`), Mean $\bar{A} = \mathbf{-0.002853}$ (`-0.003`), Mean $\bar{\Delta} = \mathbf{+0.686617}$ (`+0.687`).
- **All 18 Evaluations (3 methods $\times$ 6 cohorts):** Mean $\bar{S} = \mathbf{0.638304}$ (`0.638`), Mean $\bar{A} = \mathbf{0.045733}$ (`0.046`), Mean $\bar{\Delta} = \mathbf{+0.592571}$ (`+0.593`).
